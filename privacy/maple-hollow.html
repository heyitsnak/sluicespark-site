Maple Hollow privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Last updated November 12, 2026

Maple Hollow is a single-player idle game for Android, published by SluiceSpark LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: we don't collect any.

At a glance

  • No account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • We run no server, and the game sends nothing to us.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play.

Information we collect

None. Maple Hollow does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking software.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your save: game progress, stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your settings: choices such as theme, number format, sound, music, haptics and reminders.
  • Your Spuds and Spud boons: the in-game currency and the boons bought with it, kept on your device apart from your save.
  • A crash note: if the game crashes, it saves a short technical error report on your device and shows it the next time you open the game. It is never sent automatically. If you choose to copy it and email it to us, we use it only to fix the problem.

You can erase your progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress. Reset progress keeps your purchases, Spuds and Spud boons. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings erases everything, Spuds included.

Notifications

The game can schedule reminders on your device, for example when a power is ready or your offline time is about to run out. They are created and shown locally by Android, and nothing is sent anywhere. On Android 13 and later the game asks for notification permission once. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in Android's settings.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. There are two kinds:

  • Unlocks are bought once. The game asks Google Play, on your device, which unlocks your Google account owns, so it can turn them on and restore them after a reinstall.
  • Spud packs are consumable. The Spuds are added to the balance on your device, and the purchase is then used up. Google Play can't restore Spuds: after a reinstall or on a new phone, they come back only if Android backup restored the game's data.

We receive nothing about you from either.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your device may back up the game's save to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and we can't see it. The crash note is excluded from backups.

Children

Maple Hollow collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything the game stores is on your device and under your control. If you have a question about your privacy, including under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, email us and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds a feature that collects data, such as an online feature, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
